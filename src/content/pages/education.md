## Introduction

Personalized music lessons and consultations designed to help you develop your skills, understand your instrument, and make better music.

## Lessons & Consulting

### Guitar

Technique, musicianship, songwriting, tone, and practical playing.

### Bass

Groove, technique, theory, tone, and playing within an ensemble.

### Drums

Fundamentals, groove, coordination, dynamics, and musicality.

### Piano / Keys

Chords, harmony, accompaniment, songwriting, and practical keyboard skills.

### Singing

Technique, musicianship, performance, vocal development, and healthy practice.

### Production & Mixing

Recording, arrangement, production, mixing, effects, and workflow.

### Consultation

One-on-one guidance for artists, musicians, producers, and developing projects.
